## 🎯 Phase Objective

Phase 05 will determine whether the important relationships observed during EDA are statistically supported.

The goal is **not** to run many statistical tests.

The goal is to validate only the relationships that have clear analytical and HR/business relevance.

EDA Observation
      ↓

      ↓
Interpretation

Keep / Reject / Investigate
